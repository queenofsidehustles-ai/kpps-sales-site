Your Website Setup Guide
Step by step — simple enough for anyone to follow
Welcome! As part of your Kids Party Profit System package, Coach Monica has built your website files and sent them to you. This guide walks you through everything — from creating your free account, adding your photos, uploading your site, connecting your booking form, and adding your payment link. Follow each step in order and do not skip ahead. You have got this!
1
Create Your Free Netlify Account
Takes about 3 minutes — completely free
  • 1
    Open your internet browser and go to netlify.com
  • 2
    Click the Sign Up button in the top right corner of the page.
  • 3
    Choose how you want to sign up. The fastest option is Continue with Google using your existing Google or Gmail account. Or click Sign up with Email to use any email address and create a password.
  • 4
    If you signed up with email — check your inbox for a confirmation email from Netlify and click the link inside to verify your account.
  • 5
    Once you are in you will land on your Netlify Dashboard. This is your home base. Keep this tab open — you will need it in Step 3.
💡
You do not need to enter any payment information. Netlify is completely free for everything you need.
2
Add Your Photos to the Website Folder
Do this before uploading — photos must be inside the folder first
  • 1
    Locate the website folder Coach Monica sent you on your computer. It may be in your Downloads folder, saved on your Desktop, or wherever you saved it. Open the folder so you can see the files inside.
  • 2
    Coach Monica will tell you whether she needs photos from you or whether she has already included images. If she needs photos from you she will send you a list of exact file names to use — for example profile-photo.jpg or hero-image.png.
  • 3
    Find those photos on your phone or computer. Rename each photo file to exactly match the name Coach Monica gave you. To rename: right click the file, select Rename, type the new name, and press Enter.
  • 4
    Look inside the website folder for a subfolder called images or assets. If you see one — move your renamed photos into that subfolder. If there is no images subfolder just place your photos directly into the main website folder alongside the other files.
  • 5
    Once all your photos are in the folder you are ready for the next step. Do not upload yet until your photos are in place — otherwise your site will show blank spaces where images should appear.
💡
If Coach Monica did not ask you for photos she has already included everything your site needs. You can always send her photos later and she will update the files for you.
3
Upload Your Website Folder to Netlify
This is how your site goes live on the internet
  • 1
    Go back to your Netlify Dashboard tab. Scroll down until you see a box that says Want to deploy a new site without connecting to Git? with a drag and drop area underneath it.
  • 2
    You have two ways to upload — choose whichever feels easier:
    Option A — Drag and Drop
    Find your website folder on your Desktop or in your Downloads folder. Click and hold the folder, drag it over to the Netlify upload box on your screen, and let go. It will upload automatically.
    Option B — Click to Browse (easier for most people)
    Look inside the Netlify upload box for a link that says browse to upload or click to select. Click it, find your website folder in the window that opens, click on it once to select it, then click Open or Choose. Netlify will do the rest.
  • 3
    Wait about 30 seconds. Netlify will process your files and your website will go live automatically.
  • 4
    Netlify will give you a web address that looks something like random-name.netlify.app — click it to see your live website. You will customize this address in Step 5.
Always upload the entire FOLDER — not individual files. The whole folder must go in together so Netlify can find everything it needs to display your site correctly.
4
Connect Your Booking Form
So you get notified every time someone submits a booking request
  • 1
    From your Netlify Dashboard click on your site name to open your site settings.
  • 2
    In the left menu click Forms. Netlify automatically detects any booking or contact forms that are built into your website files.
  • 3
    You should see your booking form listed there. If it shows Active your form is already collecting submissions automatically inside Netlify.
  • 4
    To receive an email notification every time someone submits the form — click your form name, then click Form notifications, then Add notification, then select Email notification. Enter your email address and click Save.
  • 5
    Test it yourself. Go to your live website, fill out the booking form with your own name and information, and submit it. Check your email — you should receive a notification within a few minutes confirming everything is working.
💡
All booking form submissions are saved inside Netlify under the Forms tab so you can always log in and review them — even if you miss an email notification.
5
Add Your Stripe Payment Link
So clients can pay you directly from your website
  • 1
    If you do not have a Stripe account yet go to stripe.com and click Start now. Sign up with your email, follow the steps, and connect your bank account so payments deposit directly to you.
  • 2
    Once inside Stripe click Payment Links in the left menu, then click Create payment link.
  • 3
    Name your product — for example Party Booking Deposit or Kids Party Package. Set the price amount and click Create link. Stripe will generate a payment link that looks like buy.stripe.com/xxxx
  • 4
    Copy that payment link and send it to Coach Monica along with the button text you want on your site — for example Book Now or Reserve Your Date. She will update your website files so the button goes directly to your Stripe payment page.
  • 5
    Once Coach Monica sends you the updated files follow Step 8 below to upload them and your payment button will be live.
💡
Stripe is free to set up. They only charge a small processing fee of 2.9% plus 30 cents when you actually receive a payment. No monthly fees ever.
6
Give Your Site a Custom Name
Optional but recommended — makes your link look professional
  • 1
    From your Netlify Dashboard click on your site name, then click Site Configuration in the left menu.
  • 2
    Click Change site name, type in a name that represents your business — for example monicasparties.netlify.app — and click Save. Your new address goes live immediately.
💡
If you already own a custom domain like yourpartywebsite.com you can connect it under Site Configuration by clicking Domain Management. Ask Coach Monica if you need help — it takes about 10 minutes.
7
Check That Everything Is Working
Do this before sharing your site with anyone
  • 1
    Click your site address and confirm the page loads correctly and looks the way it should.
  • 2
    Click every button and link on your site — especially your booking form and your payment button.
  • 3
    Fill out your booking form yourself as a test and confirm you receive the email notification.
  • 4
    Click your payment button and confirm it goes to your Stripe payment page correctly.
  • 5
    Open your site on your phone and confirm it looks good on mobile — most of your clients will find you from their phones.
  • 6
    Once everything looks great add your link to your TikTok bio, Instagram bio, and Facebook page right away and start getting booked.
8
Updating Your Site When Coach Monica Sends New Files
Simple — takes less than 2 minutes
  • 1
    Log into netlify.com and click on your site name.
  • 2
    Click the Deploys tab at the top and scroll down to the upload box.
  • 3
    Drag your updated folder in or click to browse and select it — exactly the same as Step 3. Netlify replaces the old files automatically within about 30 seconds.
💡
Your site address never changes no matter how many times you update it. You can update as often as you need to.
